# Changelog

## 1.0.2 – 2026-10-10

- New positions `center-right` and `center-left`; `center-right` is the new default.
- New `Config.Offset` to move notifications away from the screen edge and clear of your HUD.

## 1.0.1 – 2026-10-10

- Default position is now `top-center`, so notifications no longer cover a HUD in the top right corner.

## 1.0.0 – 2026-10-10

- First version.
